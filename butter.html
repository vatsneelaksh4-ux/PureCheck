🧈 Butter

Detection of Starch Adulteration in Butter

Possible Adulterant

Starch

Materials Required

  • Butter sample
  • Iodine solution
  • Clean container

Procedure

  1. Take a small amount of butter in a clean container.
  2. Add 2–3 drops of iodine solution.
  3. Observe any colour change carefully.

Observation

Did a blue-black colour appear after adding iodine solution?
