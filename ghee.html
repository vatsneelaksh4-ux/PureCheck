🥛 Ghee

Detection of Vanaspati Adulteration in Ghee

Possible Adulterant

Vanaspati

Materials Required

  • Ghee sample
  • Concentrated Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)
  • Common Sugar
  • Clean Test Tube or Container

Procedure

  1. Take about one teaspoon of melted ghee in a test tube.
  2. Add an equal quantity of concentrated HCl.
  3. Add a pinch of sugar.
  4. Shake the mixture thoroughly.
  5. Allow it to stand for about 5 minutes.
  6. Observe any colour change.

Observation

Did a crimson red or pink colour appear?
