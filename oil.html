🫒 Cooking Oil

Detection of Adulterants in Edible Oil

Possible Adulterants

Impurities, suspended particles, or foreign substances.

Materials Required

  • Edible oil sample
  • Clean white paper
  • Spoon
  • Glass bowl

Procedure

  1. Take a small quantity of edible oil in a clean glass bowl.
  2. Observe the colour and clarity of the oil carefully.
  3. Take a few drops of oil using a spoon.
  4. Place the drops on a clean white paper.
  5. Allow it to stand for a few minutes.
  6. Observe the spot formed on the paper.

Observation

Did the oil leave a dark-coloured, uneven spot or show particles on the white paper?
