🌿 Turmeric Powder

Detection of Adulterants in Turmeric Powder

Possible Adulterant

Artificial colouring matter and other impurities

Materials Required

  • Turmeric powder sample
  • Water
  • Lemon juice
  • Clean glass bowl

Procedure

  1. Take a small quantity of turmeric powder in a clean glass bowl.
  2. Add some water to it.
  3. Mix well and allow it to stand for 5 minutes.
  4. Add 2–3 drops of lemon juice.
  5. Observe the colour change.

Observation

Did the water turn dark yellow immediately and did the colour spread quickly?
