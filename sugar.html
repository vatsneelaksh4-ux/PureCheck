Detection of Adulterants in Sugar

Possible Adulterant

Artificial colouring matter and other impurities

Materials Required

  • Sugar sample
  • Dilute Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) or Lemon Juice
  • Clean dry glass bowl

Procedure

  1. Take a small quantity of sugar in a clean dry glass bowl.
  2. Add 2–3 drops of dilute HCl or lemon juice.
  3. Mix well.
  4. Allow it to stand for about 2 minutes.
  5. Observe any colour change.

Observation

Did the sugar turn yellow or show any other colour after adding dilute HCl or lemon juice?
